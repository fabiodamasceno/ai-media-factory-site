AI Media Factory

A private, self-hosted automation that publishes the videos our team produces to our own YouTube channels, Próxima Era and Pausa pra Pensar.

How it works

  1. Our pipeline prepares a video, its thumbnail, title, description and Portuguese captions.
  2. A channel owner reviews it and approves it by email. Nothing is published without this approval.
  3. The approved video is uploaded to our own channel as private, with a scheduled publish time. YouTube makes it public at that time.
  4. Later, we read our own channels' analytics to plan new content.

Who uses it

Only the owners of the two channels. AI Media Factory is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It only accesses YouTube channels that we own, after the owner authorizes it with Google.

Channels

  • Pausa pra Pensar: authorized clips of podcasts and interviews, with credit to the original creators.
  • Próxima Era: explainers about technology and the future.
